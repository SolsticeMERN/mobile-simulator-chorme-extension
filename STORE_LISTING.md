## 📝 Full Detailed Description (Copy & Paste for Chrome Web Store)

**Mobile Responsive Simulator** is the ultimate all-in-one responsive web testing tool designed for front-end developers, UI/UX designers, freelancers, QA testers, and digital agencies.

Test the mobile version of any website, check your responsive web design across **81+ pre-configured devices**, and prepare client-ready deliverables in one click — all without leaving your browser and without needing a physical phone for every breakpoint!

#### ⚡ Works on Any URL & Localhost
- Test live production websites, staging environments, and local development servers (`http://localhost:3000`, `http://127.0.0.1:5173`, `http://localhost:8080`, etc.).
- Compatible with React, Next.js, Vue, Nuxt, Angular, Svelte, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and static HTML sites.
- Built-in header bypass engine effortlessly handles sites with strict `X-Frame-Options` and CSP headers.

#### 🗂️ Multi-Device Comparison (Side-by-Side)
- Display multiple devices simultaneously in a synchronized side-by-side view.
- One navigation updates all breakpoints at the same time.
- Optional synchronized scrolling lets you compare animations and layouts in real-time.

#### 🖼️ Transparent PNG Device Mockups
- Export presentation-ready screenshots with realistic device bezels (Dynamic Island, camera punch-holes, curved titanium borders) on transparent backgrounds.
- Ready to drop straight into client decks, Figma files, portfolio presentations, or QA reports.
- Clean frameless screenshot mode also available for fast technical inspection.

#### 🎥 One-Click Video Demo Recording
- Record smooth WebM videos directly from the simulator canvas.
- Perfect for bug reports, tutorial videos, and client walkthroughs.

#### 👆 Realistic Touch & Inertia Scrolling
- Simulates mobile touch gestures natively: finger pointer dot, touch drag, and inertia scroll decay.

#### ⚙️ Custom Device Creator
- Need a custom viewport or testing a specific fold/kiosk screen? Create and save your own custom device profiles with custom resolution, pixel density (DPR), and User-Agent strings.

#### 📱 Instant QR Code for Physical Phone Testing
- Generate a QR code in one click to open the exact URL on your physical smartphone on the same network.

### 🛡️ PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST
- **100% Client-Side**: All testing and simulation runs entirely in your local browser.
- **Zero Data Collection**: No browsing history, cookies, credentials, or personal data are ever collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.
- Manifest V3 compliant and adheres strictly to Google Chrome Web Store developer policies.

---

### 🚀 HOW TO USE
1. Click the **Mobile Simulator** icon in your browser toolbar while viewing any web page.
2. The simulator opens instantly in a dedicated tab with your page loaded inside the simulated device.
3. Switch devices from the catalog of 81+ models, rotate orientation, toggle multi-device comparison, or export high-resolution mockups with one click!
